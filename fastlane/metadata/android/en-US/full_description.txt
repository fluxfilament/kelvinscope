<p>Kelvinscope is an open-source app for the <b>FLIR ONE for Android</b> thermal camera (gen 3 and Pro, USB-C). It talks to the camera directly over USB, without FLIR's SDK, and reads the sensor at its native resolution.</p>
<p><b>Not affiliated with or endorsed by Teledyne FLIR.</b> FLIR and FLIR ONE are trademarks of Teledyne FLIR, used here only to say which camera the app works with.</p>
<ul>
<li>Live view at the sensor's own resolution (80×60 on the gen 3, 160×120 on the Pro).</li>
<li>Temperatures in °C: up to nine spot meters, measurement areas with Max/Min/average, Max/Min markers, isotherm.</li>
<li>Automatic or fixed temperature range with a scale bar under the picture, five palettes.</li>
<li>Visible-light overlay from the camera's second lens, with field-of-view and alignment adjustment.</li>
<li>Snapshots saved as a card with the picture, spots, scale and settings on it, plus the 16-bit counts to measure them again later.</li>
<li>Temperature log to CSV; calibration against melting ice, boiling water or a contact thermometer.</li>
<li>Interface in English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Russian.</li>
<li>No permissions and no network.</li>
</ul>
<p>Tested on a FLIR ONE gen 3 and reported working on a FLIR ONE Pro. iPhone and Lightning models are not supported.</p>
